AI Song Search

No title needed. Just say what you mean.

The hard part of requesting a song was never the requesting. It was knowing what to ask for. AI Song Search takes that away: a guest types what they actually mean, in plain English, and a list of real tracks from your library comes straight back, each one ready to preview and request.

80s Australian pub rocksomething for a 50thsongs from that Robin Williams movie

It shines at birthdays and functions where someone wants something for Mum, at theme nights and trivia, and at any table arguing about a song none of them can name. It means the people who would never normally request a song finally do. On the request page, it is the Help me pick button.

No guessing from a title, and no requesting the wrong version. Fewer regrets in the queue means a better run of music for the whole room.

Previews turn browsing into a game people enjoy playing, and the more they sample, the more they request.

Still your room

Your rules still apply

Previews change nothing about what can be requested. You choose the library, set the limits per person, and hold or remove anything from the Admin app.
