    <div class="comparison-box">
      <div class="comparison-item spotify-item">
        <div class="comparison-label">Spotify</div>
        <div class="comparison-type">One to One</div>
        <p>Each individual pays for the legal right to listen to the music (themselves). In a pub, there's numerous other people who also listen to the music, and it's these extra people that are not allowed to have a free ride off the individual who owns the Spotify subscription.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="vs-divider">VS</div>
      <div class="comparison-item ejukebox-item">
        <div class="comparison-label">eJukebox</div>
        <div class="comparison-type">One to Many</div>
        <p>Fully licensed for commercial use, covering all your customers. This is the reason that eJukebox can be slightly more expensive when compared to a Spotify subscription.</p>
      </div>
