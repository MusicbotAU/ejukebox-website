Questions

What venues ask first

Does the music play from Spotify?

No. Spotify is only where the playlist comes from. Every song plays from your venue's licensed eJukebox library, which is what keeps it legal for business use.

What if a song is not in our library?

It is simply not offered. Guests only see the songs that match your library, so nobody is promised a song you cannot play.

Do guests need anything special?

Just their own Spotify account. They sign in on your venue's request page - there is nothing extra to install.

What does it cost?

Spotify integration is an add-on to Core, and it is included in Complete. See plans & pricing.
