Your Guests' Playlists, Live in Your Venue

Sign in with Spotify and the music your customers love starts flowing into the room

The world's music, playing safely from your own library

The music your customers love is already living in their Spotify. Spotify integration connects that world directly to your floor: guests sign in, and the songs they actually care about start flowing into your venue, automatically matched to the library you already run, so there are no dead ends and no licensing headaches.

Nothing makes a guest feel more at home than hearing their own music, and nothing makes a venue feel more current than tapping the platform hundreds of millions of people use every day.

How Spotify integration works

Guests sign in

On the front page a guest taps "Sign in with Spotify" and chooses one of their own playlists, right there on their phone, with nothing to install.

Smart matching

The system instantly cross-checks every track against your venue's library using fuzzy matching, so "the song they want" becomes "the song you can actually play," ready to request with a tap.

Load a whole playlist

Behind the bar your team gets a superpower: hand-pick a few songs, or load an entire curated Spotify playlist straight into the player in a single move, queueing every matching track at once.

Where it shines

A regular who wants their personal playlist on the big system. A function or party that sends you their vibe in advance. A bartender swinging the room from chilled dinner service to peak-hour energy with one curated list. Theme nights, private events, and loyal locals who feel genuinely recognised.

It is the personal magic of a private DJ delivered at the scale of the world's biggest music service, all playing safely from your own library. It turns passive background music into a reason people choose your room over the one down the road.
