faqs:
  - q: Do I need a music licence to play music in my pub?
    a: Yes. Australian venues need a OneMusic Australia licence to play recorded music in public, and you hold that licence yourself. Because eJukebox is a commercial background music supplier your OneMusic tier is lower than it would be on a personal streaming account, and for pubs and clubs we can collect the fee and remit it directly to OneMusic.

    a: Yes, interactive song requests are at the heart of eJukebox. Patrons request from their own phone and you keep full control over what actually plays.
