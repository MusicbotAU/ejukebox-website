Any staff member records a quick message in their own voice, and it plays neatly after the current song. The simplest way to make a real announcement without grabbing a microphone.

Type a Message

Your own radio announcer, on demand.

Type a line and a warm, broadcast-quality voice reads it across the room. Word-perfect every time, and nobody on the team has to perform.

Type it in the Admin app, tap Hear it first to hear exactly how it will sound, then send it. It plays as soon as the current song finishes.

Scheduled Announcements

Set it once. It never misses.

Book a typed or recorded message to play itself every day, on the nights you choose, or just once. The announcements that sell are the ones a busy team forgets - this makes sure they happen.

How it works

Pick the message, the time and how often. The player fires it on time, every time, without doubling up.

Add-on

Voice Dedications

Their voice, right before their song.

When customers request a song they can record a dedication of up to 8 seconds, and it plays across the room right before their track, blending into the intro like a radio DJ. Requests get people involved; dedications make it personal.

How it works

Every dedication is transcribed and checked for bad language in real time. If it is not clean, it is rejected on the spot and never leaves their phone.

Questions

What venues ask first

Does it talk over the music?

No. Announcements and dedications drop in cleanly between songs. The one exception is Live Microphone, where the music ducks while you speak.

What stops a customer saying something rude?

Every dedication is transcribed and checked for bad language in real time, and anything that is not clean is rejected on the spot.

Do we need special equipment?

No. Staff use their own phones through the eJukebox Admin app, and everything plays through the speakers you already have.

What is included?

Recording your own announcements comes with Core. Type a Message, Scheduled Announcements and Voice Dedications are add-ons, and Complete includes them all. See plans & pricing.
