Give Your Venue Its Own Voice

Typed, recorded, scheduled and customer voice messages, played perfectly between songs

Announcements without the awkward PA

Every great venue has a voice, a tone that tells people they are somewhere that has its act together. eJukebox gives you that voice on tap. Whether you type it, record it, schedule it or hand it to your customers, every message rides the same trusted, moderated pipeline your music already runs on, dropping in cleanly the moment the current song finishes and never talking over the music.

No microphone to find, no staff member forced to perform, and no awkward dead air, just clean, confident announcements that sound like they came straight from a national radio station.

Type a Message

Your own radio announcer, on demand. Write a line in the admin panel and a warm, broadcast-quality voice reads it out across the room in seconds. It is the small detail that quietly tells every guest your venue is switched-on, modern and worth coming back to, and because it is typed, it is word-perfect every single time.

How it works

Open the Voice tab on any phone or laptop, type your message, and tap Preview to hear exactly how it will sound. One more tap sends it to the player, where it drops in the moment the current song finishes.

Where it shines

Happy-hour countdowns and last drinks. Tonight's specials and final kitchen orders. A surprise birthday shout-out, a welcome for a function, a gentle nudge to settle tabs.

Why it lands

Staff dread the microphone and audiences can hear it. Type a Message replaces the mumble and feedback with the polish of a professional broadcaster, handled instantly by anyone on the team.

Record a Voice Announcement

Say it once. Play it perfectly. Sometimes you just want your own voice, your words, your tone, over the room. Any staff member can record a quick spoken message straight from the admin page and drop it into the venue, playing cleanly after the current song. It is the simplest way to make a real announcement without grabbing a mic or interrupting the music.

How it works

Open the Voice tab, tap record and speak your message, or upload a clip you already have. Give it a listen, then send it to the player, where it queues up and plays neatly between songs.

Where it shines

Last drinks and closing time, a quick welcome, a lost-property or car-park notice, a shout-out in your own voice. Any moment that feels more personal coming from a real person.

Why it lands

Dead simple and always available, the reliable base of your venue's voice toolkit. Everyone on the team can use it in seconds and it keeps your announcements clean, clear and on-brand.

Scheduled Announcements

Set it once. It never misses. Your most profitable announcements are the ones that happen without anyone remembering to make them. Book a spoken message to play itself at exactly the right moment, every day, on the nights you pick, or just once, so your venue keeps promoting, informing and selling even when every staff member is buried in a Friday-night rush.

How it works

Create an announcement, typed for the professional voice or recorded in your own words, give it a time and a repeat pattern, daily, specific weekdays or a one-off date, and save. The player fires it automatically, punctual to the second and never double-playing.

Where it shines

"Happy hour finishes at seven." "Kitchen closes in fifteen." "Trivia every Thursday at 7:30." Recurring reminders, weekly event promos, drink and food specials and last-call messages, all on flawless autopilot.

Why it lands

On a packed night the revenue-driving announcements are the first thing to slip a busy team's mind. This guarantees they happen, perfectly timed, like a tireless promotions manager who works every shift for free.

Voice Dedications

Their voice on the big system, right before their song. Requests get people involved; voice dedications make it personal. Anyone can record a short spoken message, a birthday shout-out, a proposal, a shout to the footy team, that plays over the venue right before their chosen song. It is completely safe to hand to the public, because every dedication is checked for bad language before it can ever air.

How it works

When a guest requests a song they record a dedication of up to 8 seconds on their phone. The system transcribes it and scans for bad language in real time; if it is not clean, it is rejected on the spot and never leaves their phone.

Where it shines

Birthdays, engagements and buck's and hen's nights. Footy-club shout-outs and milestone celebrations. A regular surprising a mate, a bartender hyping a table, a whole crowd counting someone down.

Why it lands

Deeply personal and completely safe. The 8-second limit keeps it punchy and the real-time profanity scan guarantees every dedication that airs is clean, with no moderation headache for your staff.
