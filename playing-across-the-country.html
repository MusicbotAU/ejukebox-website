How it works

The hardest question a guest faces, answered

"What should I play?" stops more requests than anything else. Playing Across the Country answers it before anyone has to ask.

How it works

A live national grid

The front of your request page swaps its browse tiles for a live, updating grid of what is trending in venues nationwide over the last hour, filtered to your style. A guest taps any track and it goes straight into your queue, no searching required.

Where it shines

When nobody knows what to pick

Discovery moments when people do not know what to request, younger crowds who chase what is hot, and any venue that wants to feel current instead of stuck on the same tired playlist.

Why it lands

A room that feels switched on

Your venue feels part of something far bigger than four walls, and a screen showing the country's soundtrack updating live is a genuine talking point.
