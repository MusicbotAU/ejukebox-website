Playing Across the Country

Play what the country's playing. The front page swaps its browse tiles for a live, updating grid of what is trending nationwide, filtered to your venue's music style.

How it works

The front page shows a live grid of what is trending nationwide over the last hour, the hottest tracks, what is on repeat, what just dropped, filtered to your style. A guest taps any track and it goes straight into your queue, no searching required.

Where it shines

Discovery moments when people do not know what to request, younger crowds who chase what is hot, and any venue that wants to feel current instead of stuck on the same old playlist.

Why it lands

It positions your venue as switched-on and in-the-know, part of a national movement rather than an isolated bar, and a screen showing the country's soundtrack updating live is a genuine talking point.

Song Previews

Hear it before you request it. No more requesting a song and hoping it is the right one. Customers tap any track and instantly hear a 30-second clip, the best part of the song, right on their phone before they add it to the queue. It is "try before you queue," and it makes requesting feel effortless and fun.

How it works

Every song in the trending grid and in normal search results has a preview. One tap plays the official 30-second clip, usually the chorus, straight away, with nothing to install. Like what they hear? They request it in the same tap-flow.

Where it shines

Anyone unsure of a song's exact title or version, guests discovering new music, and busy nights where people want to sample quickly and commit. It removes every "is this the right one?" hesitation.

Why it lands

Previews turn browsing into a game people enjoy playing, and the more they sample, the more they request. A small touch that makes your whole request experience feel premium and polished.
