How Functions & Events works

Host access for the night

Give the function host their own access, just for the evening. From their phone they can skip straight to the track they want and line up exactly the right music for the moment. When the night is over, so is their access.

Clean stop for speeches

When it is time for a toast, the host stops the music neatly at the end of the current song. No fumbling, no awkward fade-outs, no dead air, just a clean pause for the moment that matters.

A polished whole night

Pair it with the photo screen showing the guest of honour and scheduled announcements ticking along, and the entire evening feels premium and personal from start to finish.
