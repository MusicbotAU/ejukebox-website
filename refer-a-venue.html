To be clear: you are not selling anything

This is the bit that stops most people, so let us be blunt about it. You do not need sales experience. You do not need to know what a OneMusic licence tier is. You do not need to quote a price, answer an objection or follow anyone up. You are not on our payroll, you are not our agent, and you are not committing to anything at all.

All you are doing is telling us about a room that could sound better, and, if you are comfortable, mentioning it to whoever runs it first. That is the whole job. The system does the rest of the work the moment somebody picks up their phone and requests a song.

  • No cost to join and nothing to buy
  • No targets, no quotas, no minimum
  • Put up one venue or twenty, it is entirely up to you
  • If the venue never signs up, that is the end of it. You owe nothing

Put a venue up

Five things, and only the venue name, the suburb, your name and your mobile are compulsory. We ask about payment later, once there is actually money owing to you.
