Questions

What venues ask first

How much delay is there?

About a second - close enough that it feels live to the room and to the person talking.

What happens to the music?

It ducks automatically the moment you start talking and comes back up when you stop. Nobody has to touch a volume control.

Do we need new equipment?

No. It runs on a staff phone and plays through your existing speakers. For the monitor, add any pair of headphones.

What does it cost?

Live Microphone and the Live Mic Monitor are add-ons, bundled in the Voice Suite and included in Complete. See plans & pricing.
