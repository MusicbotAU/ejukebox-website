Live Microphone

Tap Go Live. The room is yours. A direct line to the whole room, built for the people you trust with the mic, with no fader to ride and no sound engineer required.

How it works

A trusted staff member or MC opens the Voice tab, chooses their phone's microphone and taps Go Live. Their voice streams straight to the venue's sound system with only about a second of delay, close enough to feel truly live.

Why it lands

The music ducks automatically the moment your audio starts and swells back the moment it stops. It transforms spur-of-the-moment announcements into slick, professional ones and makes your staff look completely in command.

Live Mic Monitor

Talk up to the vocal like a pro DJ. This is the detail that separates a venue with a microphone from a venue with a show. When an announcer talks over music, the hard part is hearing your own voice echo back a beat late. Live Mic Monitor removes that echo completely, feeding the announcer the music the room hears with their own voice stripped out, so they can perform over the track like a seasoned radio DJ.

How it works

The player captures its program audio, the music with the microphone feed excluded, and streams it back to the announcer's headphones over a private channel. With no delayed voice looping back, there is nothing to trip over.

Where it shines

Talking up to the first vocal of a track without stepping on it. Teasing the next song, building tension before a drop, hosting a countdown, MCing an event with the music running underneath.

Why it lands

Sounding smooth over music is a genuine skill, and this hands it to anyone. Take away the echo and confidence follows instantly. It is the closest thing to installing a professional radio DJ behind your PA.
