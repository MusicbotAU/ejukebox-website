licensing_note: >-
  Clubs are licensed differently from pubs. Registered and licensed clubs are on OneMusic's
  scheme for clubs, which charges **per member** and already includes the Digital Copy/Delivery
  charge a pub on personal streaming pays on top - so switching music supplier won't lower your
  OneMusic fee. If your club plays only ClubMUSIC or broadcast radio today, talk to us first:
  OneMusic gives those clubs a reduced rate, and a full commercial music service moves you onto
  the standard one. Either way the licence stays in your club's name, and we can collect and
  pay it for you, at cost. [Read more about music licensing for business &rarr;](/licensing.html)

faqs:
  - q: >-
      Can each part of the club play different music?
    a: >-
      Yes. The bistro, the bar, the gaming room, the function room and the deck can each run
      their own channel on their own schedule. Core includes two zones, and one player drives up
      to four, so zones three and four are ${zone} a month each ex GST with no extra hardware.
      For more than four areas, talk to us.
  - q: >-
      Will switching to eJukebox lower our OneMusic fee?
    a: >-
      No, and we would rather say so up front. Clubs are licensed per member under OneMusic's
      scheme for clubs, which already includes the Digital Copy/Delivery charge, so changing music
      supplier doesn't change the fee. If your club plays only ClubMUSIC or broadcast radio today,
      ask us first - OneMusic gives those clubs a reduced rate, and a full commercial music service
      moves you onto the standard one. <a href="/licensing.html">How music licensing works</a>.
  - q: >-
      Can we call the raffle and the members' draw over the music?
    a: >-
      Yes. Live Microphone turns any staff phone into a PA mic and ducks the music while you talk,
      and Scheduled Announcements can remind members about the draw, happy hour, the courtesy bus
      and last drinks on their own. Both are add-ons, bundled in the Voice Suite and included in
      Complete. <a href="/live-microphone/">See Live Microphone</a>.
  - q: >-
      Can members request songs?
    a: >-
      If you want them to. Members scan a QR code and request from their phone or the free
      eJukebox Request app, or walk up to a touchscreen request booth. You decide what can be
      requested and how often, and you can switch requests off for a band, a speech or a quiet
      dinner. <a href="/in-venue-screens/">See the touchscreen and in-venue screens</a>.
  - q: >-
      Can you work with our AV contractor?
    a: >-
      Yes. We set the player up for your zones before it leaves us, each zone takes one audio
      lead to its own amplifier, and your AV contractor runs the cabling. We back it up with
      illustrated instructions and video call support.
  - q: >-
      What about function nights?
    a: >-
      Functions &amp; Events gives the function host night-only controls from their own phone -
      skip, cue the right song, stop cleanly for speeches - and switches itself off when the
      night ends. Guests join in with the function's own code.
      <a href="/functions-events/">See functions &amp; events</a>.
  - q: >-
      Can eJukebox play the Ode at 6pm?
    a: >-
      Yes, two ways. Schedule it yourself - Scheduled Announcements plays the Ode every evening at
      6pm, on its own - or we build it into your club's music, with the minute's silence, as part
      of a custom branded station. <a href="/voice-announcements/">See scheduled announcements</a>.
