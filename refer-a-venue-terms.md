# Refer a Venue: programme terms

These terms govern the eJukebox "Refer a Venue" programme operated by Musicbot
Pty Ltd (ABN 44 163 918 054), trading as eJukebox. Registering a venue means you
accept them. We may change or withdraw the programme at any time, and any venue
you registered before we do will still be honoured on the terms that applied
when you registered it.

**Nothing on this page is tax or legal advice.** A referral payment may be
assessable income in your hands. Please speak to your own accountant.

## 1. Who can take part

You may register a venue if you are **18 years of age or over** and resident in
Australia, and you are **not** any of the following:

- an employee, contractor or director of Musicbot Pty Ltd, or a member of their
  immediate family;
- a party to an existing eJukebox reseller, wholesale or distribution agreement,
  where the venue would fall within that agreement and is already rewarded
  through margin.

If you are **employed by the venue you are referring**, or you are otherwise
involved in that venue's decision to buy, you must tell the owner or licensee
about the payment **before** you register it. This is not a formality. Accepting
a payment to influence your employer's purchasing decision without their
knowledge is a criminal offence in most Australian states. We will copy the
venue's owner on any payment made in these circumstances.

## 2. What counts as a referral

A referral qualifies only if **all** of the following are true.

1. **You registered the venue before we contacted it.** We check the date and
   time of your registration against our own records. A venue already in our
   pipeline, already trialling, or already in conversation with us does not
   qualify, no matter who mentions it first.
2. **The venue is a new eJukebox customer.** A rename, relocation, change of
   licensee, reinstatement of a former customer, or an additional site belonging
   to an existing customer does not qualify.
3. **The venue installs and pays.** Payment obligations arise only once the
   venue has paid real invoices. See section 4.
4. **You gave accurate details.** Registering venues you have no genuine
   knowledge of, in bulk or speculatively, disqualifies every registration in
   the batch.

Where a venue is registered by more than one person, the reward goes to whoever
registered it first. Only one reward is paid per venue.

A registration for a venue we have not yet contacted stays claimable for
**{{ ref.tip_window }} days**. If the venue signs up within that window, you are
paid whether or not we came to them through you.

## 4. When we pay, and when we do not

We pay on customers who stay, not on installations. eJukebox has no lock-in
contract, so an installation on its own is not yet a customer.

- The **first payment** is made after the venue has paid its
  {{ ref.qualify_first }}rd monthly invoice in full.
- On a full venue system the **second payment** is made after the
  {{ ref.qualify_second }}th paid month.
- The venue's account must be **current, and free of any outstanding refund,
  chargeback or dispute**, on the date a payment falls due.
- If the venue cancels before a milestone, that payment simply does not fall
  due. **We do not claw back a payment already made.**
- Payments are made within 30 days of the milestone being reached.

## 5. How we pay you

You choose when the first payment falls due, not when you register:

- **Bank transfer**, if you provide a valid ABN and either invoice us or accept
  a recipient created tax invoice from us.
- **A prepaid card**, where you do not have an ABN. Australian tax law requires
  us to withhold 47% from a cash payment to a supplier who does not quote an
  ABN, so this is how we pay individuals without one. We may instead accept a
  completed ATO "Statement by a supplier" form and pay by transfer.
- **Account credit**, if you are an existing eJukebox customer.

We will never ask you for a payment, a deposit, or a fee of any kind to take
part in this programme.

## 6. What you are, and what you are not

You are making an introduction. You are **not** an employee, agent, contractor,
partner or representative of eJukebox, and you have no authority to act for us.

Specifically, you must not quote prices, negotiate terms, make promises about
what eJukebox does, describe our music licensing arrangements, use our branding,
or present yourself as working for or with us. Doing any of these things
disqualifies the referral, because it exposes both you and the venue to
representations we cannot stand behind.

There is no target, no quota, no minimum and no exclusivity. You are free to
register one venue or none.

## 7. Your information, and the venue's

We collect the details you provide in order to contact the venue, to track your
referral, and to pay you. We will tell the venue that you introduced them,
because they will reasonably ask. Do not register a venue if you would not want
them to know it came from you.

Your information is handled in accordance with our
[Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).

## 8. General

We may vary or end the programme, or decline any individual referral, at our
discretion. Where we decline one we will tell you why. These terms are governed
by the law of New South Wales.
